About the science
  • Near versus far transfer. People reliably improve at what they practise and at similar tasks. A major review found little evidence that brain games improve everyday life (Simons et al., 2016).
  • Dose. The studies cited here used about 2.5 to 35 hours of training spread over weeks. ACTIVE's long-term effects depended on booster sessions.
  • Retest effects. Scores rise just from repeating a task, so rising in-game numbers are mostly task learning. Trials compare against control groups for this reason.
  • Commercial ties. Posit Science (BrainHQ) and Akili (EndeavorRx) have financial interests in some of the studies cited.
  • Original designs. Comet Watch, Lantern Glide and Switchboard are new games built on long-published research paradigms: the useful field of view, pursuit tracking with go/no-go, and parity/magnitude task switching. They are not copies of any commercial product.
  • This is an independent project, not a medical device, and not a tested treatment.
